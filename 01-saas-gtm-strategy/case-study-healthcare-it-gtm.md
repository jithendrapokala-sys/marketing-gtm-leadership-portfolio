# Case Study — Global GTM for Healthcare IT Products

**Organization:** InterScripts · **Role:** Senior Director – Marketing, reporting to the CEO · **Period:** Aug 2025 – Jan 2026

## Context

InterScripts offers Healthcare IT solutions in EHR optimization, interoperability and AI. I owned global marketing, brand architecture and demand generation for two flagship solutions, including **Bytepad**. The objectives were to build an enterprise pipeline and to open U.S. Federal opportunities.

## Challenges

- Complex technical capabilities had to be explained in the language of executive buyers.
- Federal and enterprise healthcare buyers have long cycles and large buying committees, and they rely heavily on trust and credibility.
- Marketing had to be clearly accountable to revenue targets.
- A lean team had to do research-heavy, account-specific outreach at scale.

## What I Did

| Area | Actions |
|---|---|
| **Positioning** | Translated EHR optimization and AI capabilities into executive buyer narratives; built brand architecture across the two flagship solutions |
| **Market presence** | Positioned the company across CHIME forums, healthcare interoperability events and global industry networks |
| **Demand generation** | Ran targeted event campaigns and executive outreach aimed at enterprise and U.S. Federal buyers |
| **Revenue alignment** | Partnered with the CEO, Sales and Product to align marketing OKRs with revenue targets |
| **Enablement** | Created proof-point decks, ROI tracking dashboards and AI/CX enablement programs for cross-functional teams |
| **Automation** | Built the company's centralized AI repository and automated marketing workflows from scratch, using n8n, Python, APIs, LLMs, LangChain and LangGraph, including custom Python landing pages and CRM/enrichment workflows |

## Results

- **~$3.5M+** qualified enterprise pipeline
- **Strong U.S. Federal project interest** from event campaigns and executive outreach
- **~60%** faster campaign launch and account research
- **~40%** less manual outreach work

## Lessons I Bring to the Next Role

1. In regulated, high-trust markets, **industry presence is a demand channel**: forums and events open executive conversations that cold outreach can't.
2. **Automation multiplies a small team.** Automated research and enrichment let a lean team run account-specific outreach at enterprise scale.
3. **Shared OKRs end the MQL debate.** When marketing reports on pipeline and revenue with Sales, priorities stay aligned.
