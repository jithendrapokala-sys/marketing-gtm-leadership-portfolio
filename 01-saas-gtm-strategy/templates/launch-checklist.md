# 90-Day Product Launch Checklist

## Phase 1 — Readiness (Days 1–30)

**Strategy**
- [ ] ICP and buying committee signed off by Sales and Product
- [ ] Positioning canvas and message house approved
- [ ] Pricing, packaging and entry offer (pilot / assessment) finalized
- [ ] Launch goals set: pipeline target, meetings target, CAC ceiling

**Assets**
- [ ] Website product page + conversion-optimized landing page
- [ ] Sales deck, one-pager, battlecard, ROI calculator
- [ ] Demo script and recorded demo
- [ ] 3 launch content pieces (blog, explainer, executive POV)

**Systems**
- [ ] CRM campaign, lead source and UTM conventions set up
- [ ] Lead scoring and routing rules live (see [04 — Lead Generation](../../04-lead-generation/))
- [ ] Launch dashboard built (see [02 — Marketing Analytics](../../02-marketing-analytics/))
- [ ] Automation tested end to end: form → enrichment → CRM → owner alert

## Phase 2 — Soft Launch (Days 31–60)

- [ ] Brief and train the sales team; run practice pitch sessions
- [ ] Launch to design partners and existing customers
- [ ] Start Tier 1 ABM: executive outreach to named accounts
- [ ] Gather first proof points and quotes
- [ ] Refine messaging based on call feedback

## Phase 3 — Market Launch (Days 61–90)

- [ ] Press release / announcement + social launch
- [ ] Paid campaigns (LinkedIn, search, retargeting)
- [ ] Industry event or webinar
- [ ] Tier 2 and Tier 3 programs + nurture sequences live
- [ ] Weekly pipeline review with Sales; reallocate budget at Day 75
