# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Buying Committee Template

> Copy and fill this in for each product or segment. Review it every quarter against closed-won and closed-lost data.

## A. Account-Level ICP

| Attribute | Definition | Example (illustrative) |
|---|---|---|
| Industry / vertical | | Hospitals & health systems |
| Company size | Employees / revenue / beds / users | 500+ beds |
| Geography | | United States |
| Technology environment | Systems they run today | Major EHR platform in place |
| Buying triggers | Events that create urgency | EHR migration, regulatory deadline, merger |
| Pain points | Business problems we solve | Data silos, manual reporting, clinician time |
| Disqualifiers | When **not** to pursue | No budget cycle within 12 months |

## B. Buying Committee

| Role | Typical title | What they care about | Message angle | Content that works |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer | CIO / CFO | ROI, risk, total cost | Cost reduction, payback period | ROI calculator, peer case study |
| Champion | Director / VP | Making their initiative succeed | Quick wins, visibility | Pilot plan, internal pitch deck |
| Technical evaluator | Architect / IT lead | Integration, security | Standards, APIs, compliance | Technical brief, demo |
| End-user leader | Ops / clinical lead | Daily workflow | Time saved, ease of use | Product walkthrough |
| Procurement | Procurement / contracts | Terms, vendor risk | Flexible commercial models | Security pack, references |
