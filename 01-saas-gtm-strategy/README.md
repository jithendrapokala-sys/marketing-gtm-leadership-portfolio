## The Principles Behind It

1. **Start from the buyer's problem, not the feature list.** Complex products (EHR optimization, interoperability, AI) only sell when they're translated into the buyer's business language.
2. **Marketing owns pipeline, not just leads.** Marketing OKRs are tied to revenue targets with Sales and Product, and reviewed together.
3. **Focus beats coverage.** A narrow ICP and a named-account list beat broad campaigns in enterprise and public-sector markets.
4. **Automate the repeatable work.** Account research, enrichment and campaign set-up are automated so the team's time goes to strategy and relationships.
5. **Commercial models are part of GTM.** Where it fits, outcome-based revenue-share and gain-share structures lower the barrier for the buyer.
