# Prompt — Content Repurposing

**Inputs:** transcript or text of a webinar, whitepaper or article; target persona; brand voice guide
**Output:** a multi-channel content pack

```text
Brand voice: {{voice guide summary}}
Target persona: {{persona}}

From the source content below, create:
1. A 600-word blog post with H2 subheadings and one clear call to action
2. Three LinkedIn posts (under 150 words each): one insight, one data point, one question
3. A nurture email (under 150 words) linking to the blog
4. Five sales talking points

Rules:
- Use only ideas and data present in the source. Quote numbers exactly.
- Keep the brand voice. No clichés ("game-changer", "revolutionize").

Source:
{{source content}}
```
