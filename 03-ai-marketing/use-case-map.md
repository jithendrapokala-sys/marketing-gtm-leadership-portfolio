# AI Use-Case Map for GTM

Rated **Value** (impact on pipeline or cost) and **Effort** (data, integration, risk). Start with high value and low effort.

| Funnel stage | Use case | How it works | Value | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Research | **Account research briefs** | Agent gathers company news, filings, job posts and tech signals into a one-page brief per target account | High | Low |
| Research | Buying-committee mapping | Finds likely roles and decision-makers for an account and suggests the message angle for each | High | Medium |
| Targeting | Intent & signal scoring | Combines engagement and external signals into a priority score for each account | High | Medium |
| Messaging | Persona-specific outreach drafts | Drafts first-touch emails per persona from the message house; a human edits and sends | High | Low |
| Content | Content repurposing | Turns one webinar or whitepaper into blog, social posts, email and talking points | Medium | Low |
| Content | SEO content briefs | Keyword clusters, search intent, outline, FAQs | Medium | Low |
| Campaigns | Landing page variants | Generates copy variants for A/B tests; Python builds the pages | Medium | Medium |
| Sales enablement | Call & objection summaries | Summarizes calls and logs objections to update battlecards | Medium | Medium |
| RevOps | CRM data hygiene | Normalizes titles, de-duplicates records, fills missing firmographics | Medium | Low |
| Analytics | Natural-language reporting | Answers "why did SQLs drop?" from dashboard data | Medium | High |

## Where AI Should **Not** Act Alone

- Final outreach to Tier 1 executives: always human-reviewed
- Pricing, contract or compliance statements
- Anything involving regulated data (e.g. patient data in healthcare)
