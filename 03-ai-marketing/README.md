# 03 — AI Marketing

AI is most useful in GTM when it removes slow, repetitive work, such as account research, enrichment, first drafts and campaign set-up, so people can spend their time on judgment and relationships. This project shows where I apply AI, how I structure it, and how I keep it safe and on-brand.
