# AI Governance for Marketing

| Area | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Data privacy** | No personal, customer-confidential or regulated (e.g. patient) data goes into external AI tools unless an approved, contracted platform is used. |
| **Accuracy** | Every factual claim in AI output must trace to a source in the knowledge layer or a cited public source. Numbers are never invented. |
| **Brand voice** | Prompts include the brand voice guide; outputs are checked against it. |
| **Human review** | Humans approve all external content, all Tier 1 outreach, and anything mentioning pricing, compliance or legal terms. |
| **Originality** | Content is checked for plagiarism; AI drafts are a starting point, not the final piece. (I wrote about this in *The Creative Renaissance: Overcoming Automation and Plagiarism in Content Writing*.) |
| **Transparency** | Teams know which workflows use AI and where it's logged. |
| **Measurement** | Each workflow reports time saved and quality metrics; workflows that don't earn their keep are retired. |
