# Account-Based Marketing Playbook

## Three Tiers

| | **1:1 (Strategic)** | **1:few (Cluster)** | **1:many (Programmatic)** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounts | 10–25 | 50–200, grouped by vertical or pain | 500+ |
| Personalization | Account-specific | Cluster-specific | Persona / industry |
| Content | Custom POV, account-specific ROI case | Vertical case studies, roundtables | Targeted ads, nurture |
| Channels | Executive outreach, events, direct mail, custom landing page | LinkedIn, webinars, inside sales | LinkedIn / display, email |
| Owner | AE + marketer as a pod | ABM marketer + SDR team | Demand-gen team |

## 6-Step Process

1. **Select accounts:** ICP fit + intent signals + Sales input
2. **Research:** account brief and buying-committee map (automated with AI, see [03 — AI Marketing](../03-ai-marketing/use-case-map.md))
3. **Plan:** message per persona, offer, channel plan, and sales plays per account or cluster
4. **Orchestrate:** marketing air-cover (ads, content) runs together with sales outreach (email, calls, LinkedIn)
5. **Engage executives:** forums, roundtables, executive-to-executive outreach
6. **Measure and adjust:** engaged contacts per account, meetings, opportunities, pipeline, win rate vs. non-ABM

## ABM Metrics

| Stage | Metric |
|---|---|
| Reach | % of target accounts reached; contacts reached per account |
| Engagement | Engaged contacts per account; account engagement score trend |
| Pipeline | Opportunities and pipeline from target accounts |
| Revenue | Win rate and deal size vs. non-ABM deals; sales cycle length |
