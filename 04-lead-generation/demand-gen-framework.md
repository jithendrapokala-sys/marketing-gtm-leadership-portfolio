# Full-Funnel Demand Generation Framework

```
  AWARENESS          ENGAGEMENT           CONVERSION          ACCELERATION
  ─────────          ──────────           ──────────          ────────────
  SEO & content      Webinars & events    Demo / assessment   Sales enablement
  LinkedIn & Meta    Gated content        Inside sales calls  ABM air-cover on
  Industry forums    Nurture sequences    Landing pages + CRO  open opportunities
  PR & thought        Retargeting          Lead scoring &      Proof points & ROI
   leadership                               fast routing        tools
```

## 1. Foundations (before spending a dollar)

- ICP and tiered account list ([GTM playbook](../01-saas-gtm-strategy/gtm-playbook.md))
- Lead stage definitions and follow-up SLA agreed with Sales
- Scoring and routing live in CRM ([lead scoring](lead-scoring-model.md))
- Tracking: UTMs, CRM campaigns, GA4 conversions

## 2. Program Mix by Motion

| Motion | Target | Core programs | Primary KPI |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ABM** | Tier 1–2 named accounts | Executive outreach, LinkedIn ads, events, direct mail | Account engagement, opportunities |
| **Inbound** | ICP-fit searchers | SEO, content, CRO, webinars | MQL → SQL rate, cost per SQL |
| **Outbound** | ICP lists | Inside sales email + warm calling sequences | Meetings held, SQLs |
| **Paid** | Look-alikes & retargeting | LinkedIn, Meta, search | Cost per SQL, pipeline per $ |
| **Events & forums** | Industry buyers | Sponsorships, speaking, hosted dinners | Executive meetings, pipeline |

## 3. Inside Sales as a Demand Engine

From leading inside sales teams at Dell, GSS Infotech, Eli Research, Tata and AJR:

- **Speed to lead:** respond to hot leads within hours, not days
- **Multi-touch cadence:** email, call and LinkedIn over 2–3 weeks, about 8–12 touches
- **Warm calling:** call contacts who've already engaged, with a reason specific to them
- **Daily metrics:** connects, conversations and meetings booked; weekly coaching on call quality

## 4. Conversion Rate Optimization

- One goal per landing page; the form asks only for what routing needs
- Social proof near the call to action: logos, numbers, quotes
- A/B test headline and offer before design details
- Track form-start vs. form-complete to find friction

## 5. Optimize

Weekly program review → monthly budget shift toward the best **pipeline per $** → quarterly ICP and scoring recalibration using won and lost deals. See [02 — Marketing Analytics](../02-marketing-analytics/).
