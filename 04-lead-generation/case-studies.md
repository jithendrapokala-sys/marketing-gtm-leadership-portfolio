## 1. Dell Technologies — Large-Scale Revenue & Social Selling

**Role:** Sales Support Supervisor · Jul 2003 – Jul 2012

- Led **195+ sales professionals** (175 acquisition, 20 retention) selling Dell infrastructure solutions across **North America and EMEA**
- Delivered **$16M USD** in annual offline and online revenue
- Scaled a dedicated **social media sales unit generating $10M USD a year**

**What it taught me:** Running demand at scale depends on daily metrics, coaching and process discipline. New channels like social selling can become major revenue lines when they're treated as a real sales motion.

## 4. BuyOnlineCrackers.in — SEO-Led E-commerce Venture

**Role:** Founder · 2013 – 2015 (alongside corporate roles, full-time 2014–2015)

- Launched and scaled an e-commerce portal **driven purely by technical and content SEO**
- Reached **#2 search engine rankings** in 2014
- Captured **30,000 qualified product inquiries from 45,000 targeted visitors**

**What it taught me:** Organic search can be a low-CAC demand engine when content matches search intent exactly. Getting the targeting right drives conversion more than raw traffic does.
