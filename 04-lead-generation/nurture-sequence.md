# B2B Email Nurture Sequence (6 Touches)

**Audience:** ICP-fit leads that aren't sales-ready yet (grade C)
**Goal:** move them to MQL by teaching them something useful, not by pushing them
**Exit rules:** stop the sequence when the lead books a meeting, reaches MQL, or unsubscribes

| # | Day | Theme | Content | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | Welcome + the problem | Short note naming the core pain for their persona | Read the 3-minute guide |
| 2 | 4 | Insight | Industry data point or trend and what it means for them | Download the report |
| 3 | 9 | How others solved it | Customer story with a clear before / after | Read the case study |
| 4 | 15 | Framework | Practical checklist or self-assessment | Take the 5-minute assessment |
| 5 | 22 | Proof | ROI example or peer quote | See the ROI calculator |
| 6 | 30 | Direct offer | Personal note from an expert | Book a 20-minute consultation |

## Writing Rules

- Under 150 words, one idea per email, one call to action
- Personalize by persona and industry, not just first name
- Plain-text style for touches 1 and 6 (feels personal); designed for 2–5
- Subject lines under 7 words; A/B test each touch

## Measure

Open → click → MQL rate per touch; meetings booked from the sequence; unsubscribe rate. Rewrite the weakest touch every month.
