## How I Approach Automation

1. **Map the process first.** As a Lean Six Sigma Black Belt, I measure the current process, remove waste, and only then automate. Automating a broken process just makes it break faster.
2. **Start with the money path.** Lead capture → routing → follow-up has the biggest revenue impact.
3. **Human in the loop where it matters.** Automate research and drafts; people own judgment and relationships.
4. **Measure hours saved and revenue impact,** not the number of workflows built.
