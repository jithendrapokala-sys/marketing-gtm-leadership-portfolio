# Case Study — AI-Enabled Marketing & Inside-Sales Workflows

**Organization:** Accenture · **Role:** General Manager (External Designation) – Solution Architecture Manager · **Period:** Sep 2021 – Dec 2024

## Context

Global clients, including **Google, Microsoft, Spotify, Airbnb, Meta, Meesho, Saudi Arabian Airlines, Australia Gas, a French bank and JTI**, needed marketing, inside sales and demand-generation operations that cost less and moved faster.

## What I Did

- **Designed and deployed AI-enabled workflows** for marketing, inside sales and demand generation
- **Advised senior stakeholders** across BFSI, Retail and Technology on digital growth ecosystems
- **Structured outcome-based Revenue-Share and Gain-Share commercial models** with product, technology and finance leaders
- **Led cross-functional teams** (website, social, paid media, analytics) on integrated multi-channel programs
- **Upskilled marketing practitioners** on automated signal processing and campaign analytics
- **Tools:** Python, n8n, APIs, LLMs, LangChain, LangGraph

## Results

- **~25–30%** lower operational process costs
- **~35%** faster campaign execution

## What It Shows

- Process transformation at enterprise scale for some of the world's largest brands
- Combining AI and automation with commercial thinking: lower cost to serve, plus outcome-based models that share the value created
- Building team capability, not only systems
