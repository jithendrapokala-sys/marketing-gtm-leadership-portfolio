# 06 — Thithi.in: Founder Case Study

**Role:** Founder (active venture) · **Website:** [thithi.in](https://thithi.in)

Thithi.in is a numerology-based software platform I built and run. It applies traditional Indian timing and numerology systems to business decisions such as launch dates, timing and workspace layout. As founder, I own product positioning, digital acquisition and organic growth.

This case study is about **building and marketing a product from zero**, applying the same GTM discipline I use in enterprise roles to a niche market I own end to end.

---

## 1. Positioning Challenge

The category is traditional and trust-driven, and it's crowded with individual practitioners. To stand out, the product had to:

- **Reframe the category** from personal fortune-telling to **business decision support**, aimed at founders and business owners
- **Earn trust** through methodology transparency, founder credibility and clear, structured outputs
- **Productize** a service that's usually delivered one-to-one into repeatable formats (audits, finders, reports)

**Positioning statement:**
> For founders and business owners who want more confidence in key decisions, Thithi.in is a business-focused numerology and timing platform that turns traditional methods into structured, practical recommendations. Unlike generic horoscope sites or individual practitioners, it focuses on business outcomes and explains its method transparently.

## 2. Product & Funnel Design

```
 Organic discovery            Engagement                  Conversion
 ─────────────────            ──────────                  ──────────
 SEO content           ─▶     Interactive tools     ─▶    Consultation form
 YouTube / Instagram          (timing finder,             WhatsApp / email
 LinkedIn / Facebook          audits, matrices)           Booked consultation
```

- **Website as the funnel hub**, with clear sections: platform, traditions and methodology, testimonials, founder story, contact
- **Interactive tools** as lead magnets: they show value before asking for contact details
- **Low-friction conversion** suited to the Indian market: WhatsApp, email and a short form
- **Measurement** with GA4 from day one

## 3. Acquisition Strategy (organic-first)

Applying what I learned driving 30,000 qualified inquiries purely through SEO at BuyOnlineCrackers.in:

| Channel | Role |
|---|---|
| SEO & content | Long-term, low-CAC discovery through intent-matched content |
| YouTube (@thithiin) | Explainers that build trust and show methodology |
| Instagram (thithivaar) | Daily, short-form reach |
| LinkedIn & Facebook | Business-owner audience and founder credibility |
| Referrals & testimonials | Social proof in a trust-driven category |

## 4. Tech Stack (built hands-on)

React · TypeScript · Vite · Tailwind CSS · Node/Express API · Google Gemini API for AI-generated audit reports · GitHub Actions → GitHub Pages · GA4 · Web3Forms

Building it myself keeps launch costs near zero and lets me iterate on positioning in days rather than months.
