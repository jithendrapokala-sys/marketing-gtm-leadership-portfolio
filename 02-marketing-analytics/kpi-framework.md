# Marketing KPI Framework

Four layers, each for a different audience. The board sees layer 1; the marketing team works mostly in layers 3 and 4.

## Layer 1 — Business Impact (Board / CEO)

| KPI | Formula | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Marketing-sourced pipeline | Σ opportunity value where first touch = marketing | Marketing's direct contribution |
| Marketing-influenced pipeline | Σ opportunity value with ≥1 marketing touch | Marketing's total contribution |
| Marketing-sourced revenue | Σ closed-won value, marketing-sourced | The number that matters most |
| Pipeline coverage | Open pipeline ÷ revenue target for the period | Is there enough pipeline to hit the number? |

## Layer 2 — Efficiency (CEO / CFO)

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| CAC | (Marketing + sales cost) ÷ new customers |
| CAC payback (months) | CAC ÷ (monthly recurring revenue per customer × gross margin) |
| Pipeline per $ | Pipeline created ÷ marketing spend |
| Marketing ROI | Attributed revenue ÷ marketing spend |
| LTV : CAC | Customer lifetime value ÷ CAC (target ≥ 3:1) |

## Layer 3 — Funnel Health (CMO / Sales leadership)

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| Stage conversion | Records reaching stage N+1 ÷ records at stage N |
| Lead → SQL rate | SQLs ÷ leads |
| Win rate | Won ÷ (won + lost) opportunities |
| Sales cycle length | Average days from opportunity created to closed-won |
| Pipeline velocity ($/day) | (# opportunities × win rate × average deal size) ÷ cycle length |
| Speed to lead | Time from form submission to first sales touch (SLA) |

## Layer 4 — Program & Operations (Marketing team)

| KPI | Formula |
|---|---|
| Cost per MQL / SQL | Program spend ÷ MQLs or SQLs |
| Account engagement | Engaged buying-committee contacts per target account |
| Content conversion | Form fills ÷ unique visitors per asset |
| Campaign launch time | Days from brief to live |
| Hours saved by automation | Manual hours before − after, per process |

## Lead Stage Definitions (agreed with Sales)

| Stage | Definition |
|---|---|
| Lead | Any identified contact |
| MQL | Meets ICP fit **and** engagement threshold (see [lead scoring](../04-lead-generation/lead-scoring-model.md)) |
| SAL | Sales accepted within SLA (e.g. 24 hours) |
| SQL | Confirmed need, authority, timeline in discovery |
| Opportunity | Qualified deal with value and close date in CRM |

## Attribution

- **Executive view:** first-touch (sourced) + multi-touch (influenced), shown side by side
- **Optimization view:** W-shaped (first touch, lead creation, opportunity creation) for B2B cycles
- No single attribution model is "true". Use them to compare, not to argue.
